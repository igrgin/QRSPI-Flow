---
name: qrspi-research
description: Perform evidence-first codebase research for an issue without proposing fixes. Use during the Research phase to map files, data flow, invariants, and test surfaces.
---

Read `issue.md` and produce `artifacts/research.md`.

Hard constraints:
- Do not propose implementation steps.
- Do not suggest architecture changes.
- Focus on evidence with file and line references.

Structure output:
- relevant files
- runtime/data flow
- invariants
- unknowns
- test surfaces
